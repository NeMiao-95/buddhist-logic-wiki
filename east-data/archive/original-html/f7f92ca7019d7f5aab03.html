Arcaṭa (730-790)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

Also Bhaṭṭārcaṭa (Hetubinduṭīkāloka [Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949 p. 233,3]; Dharmottarapradīpa [Malvania, ed. 1955 p. 75,19, 90,14]) and Dharmākaradatta (Hetubinduṭīkāloka [Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949 p. 233,7]); teacher of Dharmottara (Frauwallner 1961 p. 147); life dates ca. 730–790 in Kashmir (Frauwallner 1961 p. 148) or ca. 720–780.
