  • This relation of Chu and Franco 2012 (bib:27671) to the Jātinirākṛti was added by Patrick McAllister (an editor of this site).

Note on the full edition of Jātinirākṛti in Chu and Franco 2012

The diplomatic edition presented in Chu and Franco 2012 covers two works by Jitāri, both titled “Sāmānyanirākṛti”. The first is identical to Jātinirākṛti. See also Chu and Franco 2016.
