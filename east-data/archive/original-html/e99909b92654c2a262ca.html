Śubhagupta (720-780)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

The wrong retranslation of the name dge (b)srung(s) as *Kalyāṇarakṣita by Vidyabhusana 1909, p. 328, is unfortunately still found in more recent literature (Correction: Frauwallner 1933 p. 240; cf. Hattori 1960; Miyasaka 1959 pp. 53f.).

Kamalaśīla calls him Bhadanta Śubhagupta; representative of the Vaibhāṣika school; teacher of Dharmottara (doubtful) [according to Bu ston (cf. Obermiller 1931/1932 II p. 155)]; life dates: 720–780 (Frauwallner 1961 p. 147).
