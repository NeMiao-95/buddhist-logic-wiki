User notes on Pramāṇavārttikavṛttiṭīkā

KELLNER on Oct. 25, 2022, 5:15 p.m.:

As preserved, the commentary only concerns itself with the svārthānumāna chapter. Krasser 2003: 175n20 points to passages where Karṇakagomin states that he is going to explain certain topics in the second, third and fourth chapters. He must at least have intended to comment on the remaining chapters, but whether he actually did so remains uncertain (Krasser, “On the Ascertainment of Validity in the Buddhist Epistemological Tradition”, Journal of Indian Philosophy 31 (2003) 161–184).
