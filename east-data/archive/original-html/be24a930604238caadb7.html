Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkāraṭīkā Supariśuddhā by Yamāri

Regarding the title, see Franco and Preisendanz 2022 p. 421, n. *: "The title Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkāraṭı̄kā Supariśuddhā is provided at the beginning of the Tibetan canonical translation; the colophon of the Sanskrit manuscript, however, which was not available to us during the first phase of project, merely designates the work as a Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkāranibandha, without mention of a specific title."

Classical Translations

    • PVAṬSupŚu-t (Tibetan)

This is a commentary on

    • Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkāra (Prajñākaragupta)
