Dignāga (480-540)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

Life dates: ca. 480–540 (Frauwallner 1961 pp. 134–137; Hattori 1968 pp. 4-6). For the legend of his life cf. Obermiller 1931/1932 II pp. 149–152, Schiefner, ed. 1868 pp. 130–135.
Works: cf. Frauwallner 1959 pp. 83-84; Hattori 1968 pp. 6-11. Chronological sequence in accordance with Frauwallner 1959 pp. 135–137.
