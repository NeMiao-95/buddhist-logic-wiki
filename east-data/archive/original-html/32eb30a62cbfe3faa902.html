Reconstructed Sanskrit partial reconstruction of Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti

Muni Jambūvijayajī. 1966. Dvādaśāraṃ Nayacakraṃ of Ācārya Śrī Mallavadī Kṣamāśramaṇa: With the Commentary Nyāyāgamanānusāriṇī of Śrī Siṃhasūri Gaṇi Vādi Kṣamāśramaṇa. Edited with Critical Notes. 1: 1-4 Aras. Śrī Ātmānand Jain Granthamalā 92. Bhavnagar: Sri Jain Atmanand Sabha.

Note on the partial reconstruction of Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti in Muni Jambūvijayajī 1966

The beginning of Ara 9 pp. 738-744 is found in vol. 2 as well as in vol. 3. The first two volumes of the Muni’s edition contain, in notes and appendices, a large number of passages from the Pramāṇasamuccaya and the -vṛtti that were retranslated into the Sanskrit with great care, and in consideration of extant Sanskrit fragments.
Selections from Pramāṇasamuccaya and -vṛtti are contained in the appendix to the first volume (both Tibetan versions, Sanskrit fragments, and Sanskrit retranslations): 1.1-19, (pp. 97-120), 2.1 (pp. 121f.), 3.1-2, (pp. 123-132), 4.1-2, 4, 6 (pp. 132-134). Vol. 2 contains Sanskrit fragments and retranslations of Pramāṇasamuccaya and -vṛtti: 5.1-15c (pp. 607f., pp. 629f.), 5.25c-28 (pp. 638f.), 5.34-35 (pp. 650f.), 5.36 (S 728f.), 2.13-18 (pp. 724f.), 2.37ab (pp. 683).

Relations of Muni Jambūvijayajī 1966 to works in EAST

  1. Muni Jambūvijayajī 1966 contains fragments of the Sāmānyaparīkṣā.
  2. Muni Jambūvijayajī 1966 contains fragments of the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti.
  3. Muni Jambūvijayajī 1966 is a partial reconstruction of the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti.
  4. Muni Jambūvijayajī 1966 is a partial edition of the PSV-t.
  5. Muni Jambūvijayajī 1966 is a partial reconstruction of the Pramāṇasamuccayaṭīkā Viśālāmalavatī.
  6. Muni Jambūvijayajī 1966 is a partial edition of the PSṬ-t.
