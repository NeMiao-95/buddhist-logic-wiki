Japanese partial translation of Pramāṇavārttikapañjikā

稲見 Inami 正浩 Masahiro. 1993. “仏教論理学派の真理論: デーヴェーンドラブッディとシャーキャブッディ [Bukkyō ronrigaku no shinriron: Devendrabuddhi to Śākyabuddhi] [*Theory of Truth in the Buddhist Epistemological School: Devendrabuddhi and Śākyabuddhi].” In 原始仏教と大乗仏教: 渡邊文麿博士追悼記念論集 [Genshi Bukkyō to Daijō Bukkyō: Watanabe Fumimaro Hakushi tsuitō kinen ronshū] [*Studies in Original Buddhism and Mahāyāna Buddhism: in Commemoration of Late Professor Dr. Fumimaro Watanabe], edited by 前田 Egaku 惠學 Maeda, 85–118. 京都市: 永田文昌堂.

Note on the partial translation of Pramāṇavārttikapañjikā in 稲見 Inami 1993

pp. 89-93 Pramāṇavārttikapañjikā ad Pramāṇavārttika 2.1a-c, pp. 93-95 Pramāṇavārttikapañjikā ad Pramāṇavārttika 2.4d-5a

Relations of 稲見 Inami 1993 to works in EAST

  1. 稲見 Inami 1993 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇavārttikapañjikā.
  2. 稲見 Inami 1993 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇavārttikaṭīkā.
