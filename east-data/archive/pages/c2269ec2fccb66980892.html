tshad ma rnam par nges pa’i dka’ gnas rnam par bshad pa by rngog blo ldan shes rab

This work only deals with selected portions of the PVin. Rngog Lo systematically examines Dharmottara's commentary on these passages in the PVinT and frequently adduces also the interpretation of other Indian exegetes of Dharmakirti, such as Prajñākaragupta, Śaṅkaranandana, Śākyabuddhi and Kamalaśīla.

This is a commentary on

    • Pramāṇaviniścaya (Dharmakīrti)
