Sanskrit partial edition of Pramāṇaviniścaya, Chapters 1 and 2

Steinkellner, Ernst, ed. 2007. Dharmakīrti’s Pramāṇaviniścaya: Chapters 1 and 2. Beijing: China Tibetology Publishing House.
  • This relation of Steinkellner, ed. 2007 (bib:5173) to the Pramāṇaviniścaya was added by Patrick McAllister (an editor of this site).

Note on the partial edition of Pramāṇaviniścaya in Steinkellner, ed. 2007

Critical edition of chapters one (pratyakṣapariccheda) and two (svārthānumānapariccheda).

Relations of Steinkellner, ed. 2007 (bib:5173) to works in EAST

  1. Steinkellner, ed. 2007 (bib:5173) is a partial edition of the Pramāṇaviniścaya.
  2. Steinkellner, ed. 2007 (bib:5173) refers to the Pramāṇaviniścaya.
