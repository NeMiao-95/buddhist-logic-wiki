Tibetan full edition of tshad ma rigs par 'jug pa'i sgo zhes bya ba'i rab tu byed pa

Sems pa rdo rje, ed. 1983. “Nyāyapraveśasūtra.” In Nyāyapraveśasūtram Haribhadrasūrikṛta-Nyāyapraveśavṛttisahitam: Anuvādak evaṃ sampādak Sempā Dorje [Sems pa rdo rje], 1–34. BITS, Bibliotheca Indo Tibetica Series 6. Vārāṇasī: Kendriya Ucca Tibbati Śikṣā Saṃsthān.

Note on the full edition of tshad ma rigs par 'jug pa'i sgo zhes bya ba'i rab tu byed pa in Sems pa rdo rje, ed. 1983

Reprint of chos kyi rin chen, ed. n.d., pp. 267-271.

Relations of Sems pa rdo rje, ed. 1983 to works in EAST

  1. Sems pa rdo rje, ed. 1983 is a partial reconstruction of the Hetucakraḍamaru A.
  2. Sems pa rdo rje, ed. 1983 is a full edition of the Nyāyapraveśakasūtra.
  3. Sems pa rdo rje, ed. 1983 is a full edition of the NPS-t.
