Sanskrit fragments of Vādavidhānavṛtti

Frauwallner, Erich. 1933. “Zu den Fragmenten buddhistischer Logiker im Nyāyavārttikam.” Wiener Zeitschrift für die Kunde des Morgenlandes 40: 281–304.
  • This relation of Frauwallner 1933 to the Vādavidhānavṛtti was added from an external source.

Note on the fragments of Vādavidhānavṛtti in Frauwallner 1933

on pp. 300-301

Relations of Frauwallner 1933 to works in EAST

  1. Frauwallner 1933 contains fragments of the Nyāyamukha.
  2. Frauwallner 1933 contains fragments of the Pramāṇasamuccaya.
  3. Frauwallner 1933 contains fragments of the Vādavidhānaṭīkā.
  4. Frauwallner 1933 contains fragments of the Vādavidhāna.
  5. Frauwallner 1933 contains fragments of the Vādavidhānavṛtti.
  6. Frauwallner 1933 contains fragments of the Vādavidhi.
  7. Frauwallner 1933 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇavārttika.
