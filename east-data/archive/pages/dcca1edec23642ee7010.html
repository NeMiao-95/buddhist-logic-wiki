Jayanta (1020-1080)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

*Jina according to Cordier 1909/1915 and others for Tib. rgyal ba can; better Jayanta according to Peking 5723, 217a5, etc.: Dzayānata/-nta (cf. Ono 1993 pp. VIIIf.). Life dates ca. middle of 11th century. (cf. Roerich 1949 p. 227), but maybe already before Jñānaśrīmitra (cf. Ono 1993 p. IX and n. 49).
