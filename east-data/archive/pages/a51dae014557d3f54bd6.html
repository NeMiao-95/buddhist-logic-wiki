English partial translation of Pramāṇavārttika, 198-204

Yaita, Hideomi. 1985. “On Anupalabdhi: Annotated Translation of Dharmakīrti’s Pramāṇavārttikasvavṛtti.” Taishō Daigaku Daigakuin Kenkyū Ronshū 9: 216–199.

Note on the partial translation of Pramāṇavārttika in Yaita 1985

Pramāṇavārttika 198-204 (in part 1), Pramāṇavārttika 205-212 (in part 2).

Relations of Yaita 1985 to works in EAST

  1. Yaita 1985 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇavārttika.
  2. Yaita 1985 is a partial modern translation of the *Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti (="Svavṛtti").
