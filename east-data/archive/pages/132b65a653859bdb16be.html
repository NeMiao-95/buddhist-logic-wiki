Note on the partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha in Kunst 1939

On Tattvasaṅgraha 1364-1486 and Tattvasaṅgrahapañjikā (Anumānaparīkṣā).

Relations of Kunst 1939 to works in EAST

  1. Kunst 1939 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgraha.
