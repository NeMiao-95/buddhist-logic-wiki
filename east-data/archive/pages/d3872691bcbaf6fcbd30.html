Nyāyamukhaṭīkākāra (550-610)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

Śākyabuddhi gives his name as Mang po len pa'i bu [la sogs pa] (Pramāṇavārttikaṭīkā [rma dge ba’i blo gros (Subhūtiśrīśānti?), trans. mid-11th century] 313b2 ), *Bāhuleya[ādi] (Watanabe 渡辺 1976); life dates 2nd half 6th century.
