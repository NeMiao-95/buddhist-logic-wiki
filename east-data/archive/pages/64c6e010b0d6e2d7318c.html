*Sahopalambhaniyamasiddhi by Prajñākaragupta

Initial title of Peking 5753: Sahāvalambha, Lhan cig dmigs par nges pa; in the colophon:Lhan cig dmigs pa nges par grub pa, Cordier 1909/1915: Sahāvalambanirṇayasiddhiḥ; but cf. Pramāṇaviniścaya 1.55a. Iwata summarizes reasons for doubting this work was authored by Prajñākaragupta (Iwata 1991, part I, pp. 255f.).

Classical Translations
