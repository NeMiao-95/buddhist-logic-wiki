English discussion of Pramāṇavārttika

Pecchia, Cristina. 2015. Dharmakīrti on the Cessation of Suffering. Edited by Philip Pierce. Vol. 47. Brill’s Indological Library. Leiden: Brill.
  • This relation of Pecchia 2015 to the Pramāṇavārttika was added from an external source.

Note on the discussion of Pramāṇavārttika in Pecchia 2015

This publication discusses the structure of PV 2 and contains comments upon PV 2.190–216, according to Manorathanandin’s Vṛtti.
