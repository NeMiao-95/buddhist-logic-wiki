Tibetan discussion of Santānāntarasiddhiṭīkā

Kitagawa Hidenori. 1965. “Indo koten ronrigaku no kenkyū: Jinna no taikei [*Studies in Classical Indian logic: Dignāga’s System].” Suzuki Gakujutsu Zaidan.

Note on the discussion of Santānāntarasiddhiṭīkā in Kitagawa 1965

various comments in footnotes. Appendix A1: pp. 407-429

Relations of Kitagawa 1965 to works in EAST

  1. Kitagawa 1965 discusses the Santānāntarasiddhiṭīkā.
  2. Kitagawa 1965 is a partial edition of the Pramāṇasamuccaya.
  3. Kitagawa 1965 is a partial edition of the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti.
  4. Kitagawa 1965 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti.
  5. Kitagawa 1965 is a full edition of the PSV-t.
