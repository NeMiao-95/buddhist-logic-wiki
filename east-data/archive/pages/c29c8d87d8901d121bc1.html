Vādavidhānavṛtti by Vasubandhu the younger

The Vādavidhānavṛtti is known from some fragments recorded by Uddyotakara (Frauwallner 1933) and the enumeration of Vasubandhu's works by Shen Tai (Shen Tai n.d., p. 77b28f; cf. Frauwallner 1957 p. 104, n. 3).
