Note on the partial edition of phyi rol gyi don grub pa zhes bya ba'i tshig le'ur byas pa in Matsumoto 1980

vv. 65-82, pp. 297-289

Relations of Matsumoto 1980 to works in EAST

  1. Matsumoto 1980 is a partial modern translation of the Bāhyārthasiddhikārikā.
  2. Matsumoto 1980 contains fragments of the Bāhyārthasiddhikārikā.
  3. Matsumoto 1980 is a partial edition of the BASK-t.
