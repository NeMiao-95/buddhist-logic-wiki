Japanese partial translation of Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi

Sakai Masamichi. 2013. “Dharmottara No Setsunametsuron Kenkyū: Sattvānumāna Ni Okeru Ronshōin — Sonzaisei (sattva) — Seiritsu No Mondai [*A Study of Dharmottara’s Theory of Momentariness: The Problem of the Inferential Reason - Existence (sattva) - in the Sattvānumāna].” Indotetsugaku Bukkyōgaku Kenkyū [*Studies in Indian Philosophy and Buddhism] 20: 77–93.
  • This relation of Sakai 2013 to the Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi was added by Pei-Lin Chiou (an editor of this site).

Relations of Sakai 2013 to works in EAST

  1. Sakai 2013 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇaviniścayaṭīkā.
  2. Sakai 2013 is a partial edition of the Pramāṇaviniścayaṭīkā.
  3. Sakai 2013 is a partial modern translation of the Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi.
