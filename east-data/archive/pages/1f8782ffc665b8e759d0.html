Sanskrit partial edition of Pramāṇavārttika

Sāṅkṛtyāyana, Rāhula. 1935. “Dharmakīrtteḥ Pramāṇavārtikasya Bhāṣyaṃ Vārtikālaṃkāraḥ Prajñākaraguptasya.” Journal of the Bihar and Orissa Reseach Society: New Series 21: 1–158.

Note on the partial edition of Pramāṇavārttika in Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1935

From Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkārabhāṣya ad Pramāṇavārttika 3.304 until the end

Relations of Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1935 to works in EAST

  1. Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1935 is a full edition of the Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkāra.
  2. Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1935 is a partial edition of the Pramāṇavārttika.
