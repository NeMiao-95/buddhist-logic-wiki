Tattvasaṅgraha by Śāntarakṣita

In the first edition Krishnamacharya 1926, verse number 526 was mistakenly reserved for a verse lost in transmission. The second edition therefore counts one number lower from verse 527. Note that verse numbering was not harmonized for individual publications in this database; the divergence in counting has to be taken into consideration.
