Sanskrit fragments of Prajñālaṅkārakārikā

Bühnemann, Gudrun. 1980. “Identifizierung von Sanskrittexten Śaṅkaranandanas.” Wiener Zeitschrift für die Kunde Südasiens 24: 191–98.
  • This relation of Bühnemann 1980 to the Prajñālaṅkārakārikā was added by Pei-Lin Chiou (an editor of this site).

Note on the fragments of Prajñālaṅkārakārikā in Bühnemann 1980

at pp. 194-96

Relations of Bühnemann 1980 to works in EAST

  1. Bühnemann 1980 contains fragments of the Bṛhatprāmāṇyakārikā.
  2. Bühnemann 1980 contains fragments of the Anyāpohasiddhikārikā.
  3. Bühnemann 1980 contains fragments of the Dharmālaṅkārakārikā.
  4. Bühnemann 1980 contains fragments of the Prajñālaṅkārakārikā.
