Relations of Bhaṭṭa Śaṅkaranandana n.d. to works in EAST

  1. Bhaṭṭa Śaṅkaranandana n.d. is a full edition of the Īśvarāpākaraṇasaṅkṣepa.
  2. Bhaṭṭa Śaṅkaranandana n.d. is a full edition of the Commentary on Īśvarāpākaraṇasaṅkṣepa.
