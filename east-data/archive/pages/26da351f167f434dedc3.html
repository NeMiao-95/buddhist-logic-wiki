Note on the fragments of Pramāṇavārttikaṭīkā in Inami, Matsuda, and Tani 1992

Diplomatic edition of Sanskrit fragments with photographs of the manuscript. Pramāṇavārttikaṭīkā on the following passages of the Pramāṇavārttika (in Gnoli 1960): 45.7-26; 75.18-99.26; 102.14-104.28.
