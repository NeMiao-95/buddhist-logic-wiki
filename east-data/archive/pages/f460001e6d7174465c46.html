Muroya, Yasutaka. 2020. “Remarks on the Kundeling Manuscript of Śāntarakṣita’s Vādanyāyaṭīkā.” In Sanskrit Manuscripts in China III, edited by Birgit Kellner, Xuezhu Li, and Jowita Kramer, 243–88. Beijing: China Tibetology Publishing House.
  • This relation of Muroya 2020 to the rtsod pa’i rigs pa'i 'grel pa don rnam par 'byed pa was added from an external source.

Relations of Muroya 2020 to works in EAST

  1. Muroya 2020 is a partial edition of the VNṬ Vipañc-t.
  2. Muroya 2020 discusses the Vādanyāyaṭīkā Vipañcitārthā.
  3. Muroya 2020 is a partial modern translation of the Vādanyāyaṭīkā Vipañcitārthā.
