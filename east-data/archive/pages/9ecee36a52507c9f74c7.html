Durvekamiśra (970-1030)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

"Paṇḍita Durvekamiśra" according to Dharmottarapradīpa [Malvania, ed. 1955] 257,29 and Hetubinduṭīkāloka [Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949] 411,10.

Disciple of Jitāri according to Dharmottarapradīpa [Malvania, ed. 1955] 149,24, 257,23, and Hetubinduṭīkāloka [Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949] 411,2.

Life dates ca. 970–1030 (according to Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949 p. XIII).
