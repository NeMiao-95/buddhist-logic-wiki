Commentary on Īśvarāpākaraṇasaṅkṣepa by Anonymous

According to Krasser (oral communication), the commentary is incomplete and stops in the middle of the explanation of v. 6 and Śaṅkaranandana's commentary, so that a colophon is likewise missing. The author cannot have been Śaṅkaranandana himself, as he already refers to variant readings of the basic text.
