Relations of Yaita 1996 (bib:28152) to works in EAST

  1. Yaita 1996 (bib:28152) contains fragments of the Pramāṇaviniścayaṭīkā.
  2. Yaita 1996 (bib:28152) is a partial modern translation of the Tarkarahasya.
