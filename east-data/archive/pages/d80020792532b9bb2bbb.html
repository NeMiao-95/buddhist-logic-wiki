Sanskrit fragments of Nyāyamukha

Katsura, Shōryū. 2016. “A Report on the Study of Sanskrit Manuscript of the Pramāṇasamuccayaṭīkā Chapter 4: Recovering the Example Section of the Nyāyamukha.” Indogaku Bukkyōgaku Kenkyū [*Journal of Indian and Buddhist Studies] 64 (3): 1237–45.
  • This relation of Katsura 2016 to the Nyāyamukha was added from an external source.

Relations of Katsura 2016 to works in EAST

  1. Katsura 2016 is a partial reconstruction of the Pramāṇasamuccaya.
  2. Katsura 2016 is a partial reconstruction of the Nyāyamukha.
  3. Katsura 2016 contains fragments of the Nyāyamukha.
