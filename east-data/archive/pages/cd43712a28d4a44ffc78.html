Candra Sūri (1100-?)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

Previous name: Pārśvadeva Gaṇi, disciple of Vaneśvara Sūri, the disciple of Śīlabhadra Sūri (see colophon p. 82,9f.). Life-dates: 1st half 12th century (cf. Vidyabhusana 1909 p. 210 and Dhruva 1930 p. XXXIII).
