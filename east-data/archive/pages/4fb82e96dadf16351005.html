Sanskrit fragments of Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti

Miyasaka Yūshō. 1958. “Vasubandhu oyobi Dignāga no danpen [*Fragmente of Vasubandhu and Dignāga].” Indogaku Bukkyōgaku Kenkyū [*Journal of Indian and Buddhist Studies] 6 (2): 23–33.

Note on the fragments of Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti in Miyasaka 1958

Most of the Sanskrit fragments found by Ui and Miyasaka were reexamined by H. Nakamura in the footnotes of the Japanese translation of the Nyāyapraveśa(tarka)śāstra in Kokuyaku Issaikyō, 1958.
