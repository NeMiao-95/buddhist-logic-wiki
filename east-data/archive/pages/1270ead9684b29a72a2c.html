Prāmāṇyaparīkṣā I (*Bṛhatprāmāṇyaparīkṣā) by Dharmottara

This form of the title is found in Dharmottarapradīpa [Malvania, ed. 1955] 24,25 and is to be preferred over the form "Pramāṇaparīkṣā" in the title section of the Tibetan translation.
