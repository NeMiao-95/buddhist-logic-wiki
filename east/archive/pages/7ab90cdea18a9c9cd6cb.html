Japanese partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha

Hishida Kunio. 1970. “Tattvasaṃgraha no wayaku kaisetsu. 1 [*An annotated Japanese translation of the Tattvasaṅgraha. 1].” Nagoya Shōka Daigaku Ronshū [*Bulletin of Faculty of Commerce] 14: 227–56.

Note on the partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha in Hishida 1970

  1. Part 1: Tattvasaṅgraha 547-565
  2. Part 2: Tattvasaṅgraha 566-578
  3. Part 3: Tattvasaṅgraha 579-599
  4. Part 4: Tattvasaṅgraha 600-621
  5. Part 5: Tattvasaṅgraha 622-633
  6. Part 6: Tattvasaṅgraha 634-652
  7. Part 7: Tattvasaṅgraha 653-667
  8. Part 8: Tattvasaṅgraha 668-683
  9. Part 9: appeared in Aichi Kyôiku Daigaku Kenkyū Hôkoku (32), 1983, pp. 73--87

Relations of Hishida 1970 to works in EAST

  1. Hishida 1970 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgraha.
  2. Hishida 1970 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgrahapañjikā.
