Bibliographic Information for "Shastri 1972"

Relations of Shastri 1972 to works in EAST

  1. Shastri 1972 contains fragments of the Vādanyāyaṭīkā Vipañcitārthā.
  2. Shastri 1972 contains fragments of the Sambandhaparīkṣā.
  3. Shastri 1972 is a full edition of the Vādanyāya.
