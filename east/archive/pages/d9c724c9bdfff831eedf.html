Japanese partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha

Namai Chishō Mamoru. 1991. “TS ni okeru zense no ronshō: kōki bukkyōto ni yoru Bārhaspatya no hihan. 3, V.2: honnō to aizō to no higan [*The proof of rebirth in the Tattvasaṅgraha: Criticism of the Bārhaspatya among later Buddhists. 3, V.2: the origin of instinct, love and hatred].” Mikkyō Bunka [*Quaterly Report on Esoteric Buddhism] 174: 118–94.

Note on the partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha in Namai 1991

Part (2): Tattvasaṅgraha 1896, 1952, 1939-1946

Part (3): Tattvasaṅgraha 1974-1963

Relations of Namai 1991 to works in EAST

  1. Namai 1991 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgraha.
  2. Namai 1991 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgrahapañjikā.
