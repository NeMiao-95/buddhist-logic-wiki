Sanskrit full edition of Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti

Sāṅkṛtyāyana, Rāhula. 1938/1940. “Dharmakīrti’s Pramāṇavārttika with a Commentary by Manorathanandin.” Journal of the Bihar and Orissa Reseach Society: New Series 24–26: 349–84.

Relations of Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1938/1940 to works in EAST

  1. Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1938/1940 is a full edition of the Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti.
  2. Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1938/1940 is a full edition of the Notes/appendix to Manorathanandin's Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti.
  3. Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1938/1940 is a partial edition of the Pramāṇavārttika.
  4. Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1938/1940 is a full edition of the *Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti (="Svavṛtti").
