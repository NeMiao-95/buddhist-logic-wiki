Bhadraruci (510-570)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

According to Xuanzang a famous logician, teacher of Jayasena in Valabhī. Life dates: ca. 510–570 (Frauwallner 1961 pp. 136, 139, 140).
Works: unknown.
