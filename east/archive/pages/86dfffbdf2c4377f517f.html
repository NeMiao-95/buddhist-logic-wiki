Relations of Krasser 1991 to works in EAST

  1. Krasser 1991 contains fragments of the Prāmāṇyaparīkṣā II (*Laghuprāmāṇyaparīkṣā).
  2. Krasser 1991 is a full edition of the PP II (Laghu)-t.
