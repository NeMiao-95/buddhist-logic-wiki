Japanese partial translation of Tarkarahasya

Yaita Hideomi. 1989. “Tarkarahasya Kenkyū (I) [*The Study of Tarkarahasya (I)].” Naritasan Bukkyō Kenkyūjo Kiyō [Journal of Naritasan Institute of Buddhist Studies] 12: 75–98.

Note on the partial translation of Tarkarahasya in Yaita 1989

(I) (Sanskrit text of the pratyakṣalakṣaṇa chapter (pp. 33,1-44,8); (II) On sambandha, only Japanese translation (pp. 111,5-9; 69,13-71,12; 64,7-19), English summary; (III) Summary of content of anumāna chapter pp. 75,1-111,4; (IV) anumāna chapter pp. 80,3-83,22, Sanskrit text and Japanese translation.; (VI) anumāna chapter pp. 88,1-97,7, only Japanese translation.

Relations of Yaita 1989 (bib:5016) to works in EAST

  1. Yaita 1989 (bib:5016) is a partial edition of the Pramāṇasamuccaya.
  2. Yaita 1989 (bib:5016) is a partial modern translation of the Tarkarahasya.
  3. Yaita 1989 (bib:5016) is a partial edition of the Tarkarahasya.
  4. Yaita 1989 (bib:5016) contains fragments of the Pramāṇaviniścaya.
  5. Yaita 1989 (bib:5016) contains fragments of the Sambandhaparīkṣāvṛtti.
