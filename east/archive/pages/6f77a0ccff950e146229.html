Japanese partial translation of Pramāṇaviniścayaṭīkā

Watanabe 渡辺 Shigeaki 重朗. 1992. “Sadvitīyaprayogaḥ ni kansuru nisan no shiryō [*Materials on sadvitīyaprayoga].” Naritasan Bukkyō Kenkyūjo Kiyō [Journal of Naritasan Institute of Buddhist Studies] 15: 657–87.

Note on the partial translation of Pramāṇaviniścayaṭīkā in Watanabe 渡辺 1992

Translation of Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkāra (Sāṅkṛtyāyana 1953) 496,25-498,6.

Relations of Watanabe 渡辺 1992 to works in EAST

  1. Watanabe 渡辺 1992 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇaviniścayaṭīkā.
  2. Watanabe 渡辺 1992 is a partial edition of the Pramāṇaviniścayaṭīkā.
  3. Watanabe 渡辺 1992 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkāra.
  4. Watanabe 渡辺 1992 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇaviniścaya.
  5. Watanabe 渡辺 1992 is a partial edition of the PVin-t.
