Japanese partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha

Asano Gensei. 1991. “Tagaku setsu no atsukau Sāṃkhya eizō setsu: ‘Shinrikōyō’ to Jaina kōki ronsho [*The pratibimba doctrine of Sāṅkhya as treated by other schools: Tattvasaṅgraha and later Jaina literature].” Tōkyai Bukkyō [*Journal of Tokai Association of Indian and Buddhist Studies] 36: 64–45.

Note on the partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha in Asano 1991

Tattvasaṅgraha 292-297 pp. 62-59, Tattvasaṅgraha 301-304 pp. 57-55

Relations of Asano 1991 to works in EAST

  1. Asano 1991 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgraha.
  2. Asano 1991 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgrahapañjikā.
