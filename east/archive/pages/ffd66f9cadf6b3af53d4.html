English partial translation of Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti

Katsura, Shōryū. 2005. “The Reductio Ad Absurdum Argument in India with Special Reference to Nāgārjuna and Dignāga.” 龍谷大学論集 [*Journal of Ryūkoku University] 466: 2–18.
  • This relation of Katsura 2005 to the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti was added from an external source.

Note on the partial translation of Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti in Katsura 2005

Discussion of Pramāṇasamuccaya and Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti (svasaṃvedana) in connection with general observations on reductio ad absurdum in Buddhism.

Relations of Katsura 2005 to works in EAST

  1. Katsura 2005 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇasamuccaya.
  2. Katsura 2005 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti.
