User notes on Sambandhaparīkṣāvṛtti

May 10, 2022, 4:39 p.m. - Patrick McAllister

Steinkellner 2022 proposes that the Vṛtti was written by Devendrabuddhi rather than by Dharmakīrti. The editors of EAST are evaluating this hypothesis. See https://verlag.oeaw.ac.at/api/download/e-product/9783700188407_gesamt.pdf#page=15 for Steinkellner's reasoning. On EAST, the publication record is this one: https://east.ikga.oeaw.ac.at/bib/28635/.
