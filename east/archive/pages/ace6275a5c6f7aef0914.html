Sanskrit full edition of Hetubinduṭīkā

Sanghavi, Sukhlalji, and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949. Hetubinduṭīkā of Bhaṭṭa Arcaṭa with the Sub-Commentary Entitled Āloka of Durveka Miśra. Baroda: Oriental Institute.

Relations of Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949 to works in EAST

  1. Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949 is a full edition of the Hetubinduṭīkā.
  2. Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949 is a full edition of the Hetubinduṭīkāloka.
  3. Sanghavi and Muni Shri Jinavijayaji, eds. 1949 is a full reconstruction of the Hetubindu.
