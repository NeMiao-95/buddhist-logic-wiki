Relations of Krasser 2002 to works in EAST

  1. Krasser 2002 is a partial edition of the PVinṬ-t.
  2. Krasser 2002 is a full edition of the Īśvarāpākaraṇakārikā.
  3. Krasser 2002 is a full edition of the Saṅkṣipteśvarāpākaraṇakārikā.
  4. Krasser 2002 is a full edition of the Īśvarāpākaraṇasaṅkṣepa.
  5. Krasser 2002 is a partial edition of the Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti.
  6. Krasser 2002 is a partial edition of the Notes/appendix to Manorathanandin's Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti.
  7. Krasser 2002 is a partial edition of the PVP-t.
  8. Krasser 2002 is a partial edition of the PVṬ-t.
