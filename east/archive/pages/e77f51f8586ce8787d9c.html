Japanese partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha, 7-15

Nakada Naomichi. 1974. “Shinrikōyō oyobi sono chūshakusho ni arawareta Sāṃkhya gakuha no konponshitsuryōin ni    tsuite: wayaku narabi ni Sāṃkhyavṛtti to no hikaku [*The prakṛti doctrine of the Sāṅkhya school in  Tattvasaṅgraha and    Tattvasaṅgrahapañjikā: Japanese translation and comparison with the Sāṅkhyavṛtti].” Tsurumi Daigaku Kiyō 7–11: 161–88.

Note on the partial translation of Tattvasaṅgraha in Nakada 1974

Tattvasaṅgraha 7-15 (with Tattvasaṅgrahapañjikā).

Relations of Nakada 1974 to works in EAST

  1. Nakada 1974 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgraha.
  2. Nakada 1974 is a partial modern translation of the Tattvasaṅgrahapañjikā.
