German partial translation of Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti, 1.36-47, 2.47-52

Frauwallner, Erich. 1968. Materialien zur ältesten Erkenntnislehre der Karmamīmāṃsā. VKSKSO, Veröffentlichungen der Kommission für Sprachen und Kulturen Süd- und Ostasiens 6. Wien.

Note on the partial translation of Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti in Frauwallner 1968

Also contains a German translation of the root text Pramāṇasamuccaya 1.36-47, 2.47-52. Both translations are made based on the Tibetan translations (Cf. PS-t and PSV-t).

Relations of Frauwallner 1968 to works in EAST

  1. Frauwallner 1968 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti.
  2. Frauwallner 1968 is a partial edition of the PSV-t.
  3. Frauwallner 1968 is a partial edition of the PS-t.
