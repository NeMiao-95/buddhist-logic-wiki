Tibetan partial edition of yangs ba dang dri ma med pa dang ldang pa

Hattori, Masaaki. 1982. “The Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti of Dignāga: With Jinendrabuddhi’s Commentary. Chapter Five: Anyāpoha-Parīkṣā. Tibetan Text with Sanskrit Fragments.” Memoirs of the Faculty of Letters Kyoto University 21: 103–224.

Note on the partial edition of yangs ba dang dri ma med pa dang ldang pa in Hattori 1982

pp. 150-224: critical edition of Pramāṇasamuccayaṭīkā chapter 5.

Relations of Hattori 1982 to works in EAST

  1. Hattori 1982 contains fragments of the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti.
  2. Hattori 1982 is a partial edition of the Pramāṇasamuccayavṛtti.
  3. Hattori 1982 is a partial edition of the PSṬ-t.
