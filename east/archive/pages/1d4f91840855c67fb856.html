German partial translation of Pramāṇavārttika, 1.42-187

Frauwallner, Erich. 1932. “Beiträge zur Apohalehre. 1: I. Dharmakīrti. Übersetzung.” Wiener Zeitschrift für die Kunde des Morgenlandes 39: 247–85.

Note on the partial translation of Pramāṇavārttika in Frauwallner 1932

Pramāṇavārttika 1.42-187, together with explanations.

Relations of Frauwallner 1932 to works in EAST

  1. Frauwallner 1932 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇavārttika.
  2. Frauwallner 1932 contains fragments of the Pramāṇavārttika.
