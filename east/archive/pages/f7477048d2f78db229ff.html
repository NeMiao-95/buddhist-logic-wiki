English discussion of Pramāṇavārttika

Kellner, Birgit . 2004. “First Logic, Then the Buddha?: Remarks on the Chapter Sequence of Dharmakīrti’s Pramāṇavārttika.” Hōrin: Vergleichende Studien Zur Japanischen Kultur [*Comparative Studies in Japanese Culture] 11: 147–67.

Note on the discussion of Pramāṇavārttika in Kellner 2004

The article discusses different commentarial interpretations of the chapter sequence of the Pramāṇavārttika (Śākyabuddhi, Jayanta, also some materials from Karṇakagomin).

Relations of Kellner 2004 to works in EAST

  1. Kellner 2004 refers to the Notes/appendix to Manorathanandin's Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti.
  2. Kellner 2004 contains fragments of the Pramāṇavārttikapañjikā.
  3. Kellner 2004 discusses the Pramāṇavārttika.
