Bibliographic Information for "Yamaguchi 1953"

Yamaguchi Susumu. 1953. “Tibetan text and Sanskrit reconstruction of Ālambanaparīkṣā and            -vṛtti.” In Seshin yuishiki no genten kaimei [*Philological explanations of Vasubandhu’s Viñaptimātratā], edited by Yamaguchi Susumu and Nozawa J. Kyōto.

Relations of Yamaguchi 1953 to works in EAST

  1. Yamaguchi 1953 is a modern translation of the Ālambanaparīkṣāṭīkā.
  2. Yamaguchi 1953 is a modern translation of the Ālambanaparīkṣā.
  3. Yamaguchi 1953 is a full reconstruction of the Ālambanaparīkṣā.
  4. Yamaguchi 1953 is a full edition of the ĀP-t.
  5. Yamaguchi 1953 is a full reconstruction of the Ālambanaparīkṣāvṛtti.
  6. Yamaguchi 1953 is a full edition of the ĀPV-t.
