Japanese partial translation of Nyāyamukha

Katsura Shoryu. 1979. “Inmyō Shōrimonron Kenkyū [3] [*A Study of the Nyāyamukha (III)].” Hiroshima Daigaku Bungakubu Kiyō [The Hiroshima University Studies, Faculty of Letters] 39: 63–82.
  • This relation of Katsura 1979 to the Nyāyamukha was added by Pei-Lin Chiou (an editor of this site).

Relations of Katsura 1979 to works in EAST

  1. Katsura 1979 is a partial edition of the NM-c.
  2. Katsura 1979 is a partial modern translation of the Nyāyamukha.
  3. Katsura 1979 contains fragments of the Nyāyamukha.
