Japanese partial translation of Nyāyamukha

Katsura Shoryu. 1982. “Inmyō Shōrimonron Kenkyū [5] [*A Study of the Nyāyamukha (V)].” Hiroshima Daigaku Bungakubu Kiyō [The Hiroshima University Studies, Faculty of Letters] 42: 82–99.
  • This relation of Katsura 1982 to the Nyāyamukha was added by Pei-Lin Chiou (an editor of this site).

Relations of Katsura 1982 to works in EAST

  1. Katsura 1982 is a partial edition of the NM-c.
  2. Katsura 1982 is a partial modern translation of the Nyāyamukha.
  3. Katsura 1982 contains fragments of the Nyāyamukha.
