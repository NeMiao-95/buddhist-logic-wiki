*Prabhābuddhi (ʼod kyi blo) (690-750)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

bu ston (Obermiller 1931/1932 p. 155) says that 'od kyi blo is referred to as disciple of Śākyabuddhi. Corresponding indications by Stcherbatsky 1930, p. 40, and Malvania, ed. 1955 (Introduction p. XVIII) must go back to this source. Life dates: ca. 690–750.
Works: unknown.
