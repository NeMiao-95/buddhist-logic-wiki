Vinītadeva (710-770)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

Life dates: ca. 710–770 (t.p. Dharmakīrti, t.a. Dharmottara). Cf. in this regard: Funayama 2001. Funayama suggests he lived between Śākyabuddhi and Arcaṭa, tentatively 690–750.
