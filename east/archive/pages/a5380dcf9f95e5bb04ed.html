EAST stands for "Epistemology and Argumentation in South Asia and Tibet". This resource collects and presents bio-bibliographical information on epistemology and argumentation in South Asia and Tibet, covering a time-span from the fifth century CE until, essentially, the present day.

Users are welcome to contribute new data relating to individual texts. To do so, please register, login, and visit a page on an individual text. An "add a publication" button will appear on the left side of the page, and the rest should be self-explanatory. User-added data appears immediately on the website, but remains marked as such prior to being checked by one of EAST's expert editors.
