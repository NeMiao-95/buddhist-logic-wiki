*Vādasthānāni by Jitāri

Iyengar 1944, p. 81, suggested this title for a collection of small polemical treatises of which two, Jātinirākṛti and Digambaramataparīkṣā, are preserved completely, whereas only a part of the introductory verse is preserved of a possible third work, the *Refutation of the Pratyabhijñā system (?). Others (cf. Introduction to Thakur, ed. 1957, p. 19) refer to the collection as Vādasthāna.
