Sastri, Aiyaswami. n.d. Ālambanaparīkṣā and Vṛtti by Diṅnāga: With the Commentary of Dharmapāla. Restored into Sanskrit from the Tibetan and    Chinese Versions and Edited with English Translation and Notes with Copious Extracts    from Vinītadeva’s Commentary. 1.

Note on the full reconstruction of Commentary on Ālambanaparīkṣā and -vṛtti in Sastri n.d.

Sanskrit retranslation of Dharmapāla’s commentary based on the Chinese version, Commentary on Ālambanaparīkṣā and -vṛtti, translated by Yi Jing.

Relations of Sastri n.d. to works in EAST

  1. Sastri n.d. is a partial modern translation of the Ālambanaparīkṣāṭīkā.
  2. Sastri n.d. is a modern translation of the Ālambanaparīkṣā.
  3. Sastri n.d. is a full reconstruction of the Ālambanaparīkṣā.
  4. Sastri n.d. is a full edition of the ĀP-t.
  5. Sastri n.d. is a modern translation of the Ālambanaparīkṣāvṛtti.
  6. Sastri n.d. is a full reconstruction of the Ālambanaparīkṣāvṛtti.
  7. Sastri n.d. is a full edition of the ĀPV-t.
  8. Sastri n.d. is a modern translation of the Commentary on Ālambanaparīkṣā and -vṛtti.
  9. Sastri n.d. is a full reconstruction of the Commentary on Ālambanaparīkṣā and -vṛtti.
