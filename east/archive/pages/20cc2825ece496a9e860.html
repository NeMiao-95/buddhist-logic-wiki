Sanskrit full edition of Dharmottarapradīpa

Malvania, Dalsukhbhai, ed. 1955. Paṇḍita Durveka Miśra’s Dharmottarapradīpa: Being a Sub-commentary on Dharmottara’s Nyāyabinduṭīkā, a Commentary on Dharmakīrti’s Nyāyabindu. Patna: K. P. Jayaswal Research Institute.

Relations of Malvania, ed. 1955 to works in EAST

  1. Malvania, ed. 1955 is a full edition of the Nyāyabinduṭīkā.
  2. Malvania, ed. 1955 is a full edition of the Dharmottarapradīpa.
  3. Malvania, ed. 1955 contains fragments of the [*Nyāyabinduṭīkā]ṭippaṇaka Tātparyanibandhana.
  4. Malvania, ed. 1955 contains fragments of the Dharmottaraṭīkātippaṇaka.
  5. Malvania, ed. 1955 is a full edition of the Nyāyabindu.
