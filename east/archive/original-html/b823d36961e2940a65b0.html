Śāntabhadra (710-770)

Given dates (all in the Common Era) are approximate and represent the upper and lower possible extremes of the person's lifetime.

Cf. Introduction in Malvania, ed. 1955 p. XXV; life dates: ca. 710–770.

According to Funayama 2001 p. 318f, Śāntabhadra's commentary on the Nyāyabindu is considered to have shared much with Vinītadeva's Nyāyabinduṭīkā, and this might also be taken to suggest a proximity of their lifetimes.
