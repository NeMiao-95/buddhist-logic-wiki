Bibliographic Information for "Tani 1997"

Tani Tadashi. 1997. “Dharmottara ‘shunkanteki Shōmetsu Ronshō’ Kaisyakuno Mondai (3) [*Problems of Interpretation on Dharmottara’s Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi (3)].” Kōchi Kōgyōkōtōsenmongakkō Gakujutu Kiyō [The Bulletin of Kochi National College of Technology] 41: 59–77.

Relations of Tani 1997 to works in EAST

  1. Tani 1997 is a partial modern translation of the Prāmāṇyaparīkṣā II (*Laghuprāmāṇyaparīkṣā).
  2. Tani 1997 is a partial modern translation of the Pramāṇaviniścayaṭīkā.
  3. Tani 1997 is a partial modern translation of the Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi.
