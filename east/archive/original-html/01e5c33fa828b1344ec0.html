rtsod pa’i rigs pa rgyan gyi me tog by dar ma rgyal mtshan bcom ldan rigs pa’i ral gri

The work’s incipit gives the Tibetan title rtsod pa’i rigs pa rgyan gyi me tog and its Sanskrit equivalent Vādanyāyālaṃkārapuṣpa. For implicit and explicit references to this work in lists of bcom ldan ral gri’s works, see van der Kuijp 2003: 408–409.

This is a commentary on

    • Vādanyāya (Dharmakīrti)
