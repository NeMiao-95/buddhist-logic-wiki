Bibliographic Information for "Thakur, ed. 1957"

Thakur, Anantalal, ed. 1957. Ratnakīrti-nibandhāvalī: (Buddhist Nyāya Works of Ratnakīrti). Deciphered and ed. TSWS, Tibetan Sanskrit Works Series 3. Patna: K. P. Jayaswal Research Institute.

Relations of Thakur, ed. 1957 to works in EAST

  1. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Sarvajñasiddhi.
  2. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Īśvarasādhanadūṣaṇa.
  3. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Apohasiddhi.
  4. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi (anvayātmikā).
  5. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi (vyatirekātmikā).
  6. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Pramāṇāntarbhāvaprakaraṇa.
  7. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Vyāptinirṇaya.
  8. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Sthirasiddhidūṣaṇa.
  9. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Citrādvaitaprakāśavāda.
  10. Thakur, ed. 1957 is a full edition of the Santānāntaradūṣaṇa.
