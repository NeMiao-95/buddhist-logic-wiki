Relations of Katsura 1984 to works in EAST

  1. Katsura 1984 is a partial edition of the NM-c.
  2. Katsura 1984 is a partial modern translation of the Nyāyamukha.
  3. Katsura 1984 contains fragments of the Nyāyamukha.
